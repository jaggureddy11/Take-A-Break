How the Platform Operates

A transparent, commission-protected workflow for seekers and dudes.

For PG Seekers
  1. Post preferences: List location, budget, washroom type, and key items to inspect.
  2. Raise Escrow Bounty: Deposit ₹499 secure payment (holds on Razorpay Route).
  3. Inspect Live: Chat with your assigned Dude, review room videos, and run Wi-Fi tests.
  4. Book or Reject: If satisfied, release the funds. If details don't match, reject and get a refund.
For Local Dudes
  1. Browse Available Tasks: See list of open PG verification tasks near you.
  2. Accept & Travel: Accept the job, travel to the PG in your sector.
  3. Conduct Audit: Record raw videos, measure Wi-Fi bandwidth, audit rules, chat with seeker.
  4. Collect ₹400 Payout: Submit the verification report. Once approved, ₹400 transfers directly to your bank account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the TAB dude ecosystem.

How does the escrow payment system work? ＋

When you post a bounty, your ₹499 payment is securely held in escrow via Razorpay Route. It is only released to the Dude's bank account after they upload the verification report and you explicitly click "Release Funds". If the PG details do not match or the Dude fails to deliver, you can raise a dispute or claim a refund.

Who are the Dudes? ＋

Dudes are local freelancers, college students, or young professionals living in Bengaluru who know the city well. They are vetted by our platform for identification, communication skills, and safety before they can accept PG verification bounties.

What specific items will the Dude verify? ＋

By default, Dudes verify the exact room conditions, take raw video walkthroughs, run local Wi-Fi speed tests, audit mess food quality (ratings + photos), check water pressure/geyser functionality, and confirm the PG's curfew rules. You can also specify custom items in the special notes when posting a bounty.

Can I ask the Dude to visit multiple PGs? ＋

Each bounty of ₹499 covers a comprehensive ground inspection of a single PG. If you have 3 different PGs you want verified, you can raise 3 separate bounties. Dudes can accept them individually or in bundles.
